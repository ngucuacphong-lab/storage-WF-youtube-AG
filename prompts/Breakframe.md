# Break Frame Rules

## Purpose

Break Frames exist to interrupt the normal storytelling rhythm, regain viewer attention, and increase audience retention.

A Break Frame should create a short but powerful interruption before naturally returning to the main story.

It should feel intentional rather than random.

---

## Objective

A Break Frame should achieve at least one of the following:

- Increase curiosity.
- Surprise the viewer.
- Reinforce an important fact.
- Ask a thought-provoking question.
- Create anticipation for the next scene.
- Strengthen viewer retention.

---

## Types of Break Frames

Supported Break Frame types include:

### Type A — Question

Present an engaging question related to the story.

The question should encourage viewers to think about the topic before the story continues.

---

### Type B — Interesting Fact

Present a short and memorable fact related to the topic.

The fact should increase curiosity or strengthen understanding.

---

### Type C — Comparison

Present a quick comparison that helps viewers better understand the situation.

Comparisons should be simple and immediately understandable.

---

## Visual Design

Break Frames should be visually different from regular story scenes.

Use bold composition.

Keep the layout clean.

The message should become the immediate visual focus.

Avoid visual clutter.

---

## Color and Contrast

Use strong color contrast to immediately attract attention.

Maintain consistency with the overall project style.

Avoid colors that reduce readability.

---

## Text Rules

Keep text short and impactful.

Use language that is easy to understand.

Avoid long sentences.

Avoid unnecessary details.

Every word should contribute value.

---

## Story Connection

Every Break Frame must directly relate to the surrounding story.

It should feel like a natural interruption rather than an unrelated insert.

After the Break Frame, the story should continue smoothly.

---

## Viewer Retention

The Break Frame should refresh viewer attention without breaking immersion.

Avoid using the same style repeatedly.

Provide variety while maintaining consistency.

---

## Forbidden

Do not interrupt important explanations.

Do not introduce unrelated information.

Do not overuse Break Frames.

Do not make Break Frames longer than necessary.

Do not reduce story clarity.

---

## Break Frame Quality Checklist

Before generating a Break Frame, verify:

- Relevant to the story.
- Easy to understand.
- Visually attention-grabbing.
- Short and impactful.
- Supports viewer retention.
- Smooth transition back into the story.
