# Image Generation Rules

## Purpose

Generate visually clear, engaging, and consistent illustrations that accurately support the corresponding story scene.

Every image should immediately communicate the main idea of the scene without requiring additional explanation.

---

## Visual Style

Follow the visual style defined in the Character Lock rules.

Maintain a bright, modern, and visually appealing appearance.

Keep the overall style consistent throughout the entire video.

Avoid realistic or semi-realistic rendering.

---
## Prompt Output

Write exactly one image prompt per scene.

Each prompt describes a single still frame. Never describe motion, a sequence, or a montage.

Every prompt must end with the aspect ratio supplied at runtime, written exactly as it is given.

---
## Composition

Each image should have one clear visual focus.

Arrange visual elements to naturally guide the viewer's attention.

Avoid cluttered compositions.

Avoid unnecessary empty space unless intentionally used for emphasis.

Balance foreground, middle ground, and background when appropriate.

---

## Character Placement

The main character should naturally attract the viewer's attention.

Supporting characters should reinforce the story without distracting from the main focus.

Position characters according to the action and emotional context of the scene.

Avoid hiding important characters.

---

## Camera

Choose camera angles that best communicate the action.

Use a variety of camera shots throughout the video.

Possible camera shots include:

- Close-up
- Medium Shot
- Full Body Shot
- Wide Shot
- Bird's-eye View
- Low Angle
- High Angle
- Over-the-Shoulder
- First Person (when appropriate)

Avoid using the same camera angle repeatedly.

---

## Background

The background should clearly support the story.

Choose environments that naturally match the current scene.

Use a clean white background only when it improves clarity or intentionally emphasizes the subject.

Avoid distracting backgrounds.

---

## Environment

The environment should reinforce the story.

Include only environmental details that help explain the current situation.

Avoid unnecessary decorative objects.

---

## Lighting

Lighting should match the emotional tone of the scene.

Examples:

- Bright daylight
- Warm sunset
- Soft indoor lighting
- Dramatic lighting
- Cold lighting

Maintain clear visibility of important subjects.

Avoid overly dark images.

---

## Color Palette

Use bright and visually attractive colors.

Maintain consistent color usage across scenes.

Use color contrast to emphasize important elements.

Avoid muddy or desaturated color palettes unless intentionally required.

---

## Facial Expressions

Expressions should accurately reflect the emotions of the current scene.

Avoid neutral expressions during emotionally important moments.

---

## Body Language

Body posture should clearly communicate actions and emotions.

Avoid stiff poses.

Characters should feel alive and expressive.

---

## Visual Storytelling

The image should explain the scene even without the voiceover.

Important actions should be immediately recognizable.

The viewer should instantly understand:

- Who is present.
- What is happening.
- Where it is happening.
- Why the scene matters.

---

## Scene Readability

The primary idea of the scene should be understandable within a few seconds.

Avoid visual confusion.

Avoid unnecessary complexity.

One scene should communicate one primary idea.

---

## Visual Consistency

Maintain consistency in:

- Character appearance
- Art style
- Color usage
- Visual quality
- Overall identity

Images from different scenes should clearly belong to the same video.

---

## Image Quality

Generate clean, high-quality illustrations.

Ensure sharp lines and clear subject separation.

Avoid visual artifacts.

Avoid distorted anatomy.

---

## Creativity

Be creative in visual presentation while respecting all project rules.

Use dynamic compositions that make the story feel energetic.

Avoid repetitive layouts across scenes.

---

## Forbidden

Do not contradict the script.

Do not contradict Character_LockPrompt.md.

Do not introduce important objects that are not mentioned or implied by the story.

Do not overcrowd the image.

Do not crop important characters.

Do not obscure important actions.

Do not generate confusing compositions.

Do not reduce readability for artistic effects.

---

## Image Quality Checklist

Before completing an image prompt, verify:

- Story accurately represented.
- Main subject immediately recognizable.
- Composition is clear.
- Camera angle supports the scene.
- Background supports the story.
- Lighting matches the emotion.
- Character consistency maintained.
- Scene readable within a few seconds.
- One primary visual idea.
- No visual clutter.
