# Project Information

## Project Name
What If

---

## Project Purpose

Create highly engaging animated educational videos that explore hypothetical "What If" scenarios.

Every video should encourage curiosity by asking imaginative questions and explaining the possible consequences through logical reasoning, scientific knowledge, and entertaining storytelling.

The audience should finish the video feeling that they have learned something interesting while being entertained.

---

## Target Audience

General audience.

Suitable for teenagers and adults.

No advanced scientific background should be required.

Complex ideas should always be explained in a simple and intuitive way.

---

## Content Style

Educational

Entertaining

Curiosity-driven

Fast-paced

Easy to understand

Emotionally engaging

Scientifically grounded whenever possible

---

## Storytelling Philosophy

Every scene must make viewers want to continue watching.

Every new scene should either:

• introduce new information

• increase curiosity

• answer a previous question

• create a new question

No scene should exist only to fill time.

---

## Video Style

2D Stickman Animation

Simple characters

Bright colors

Clean composition

Easy visual understanding

Modern YouTube animation style

---

## Emotional Experience

The audience should experience:

Curiosity

Surprise

Excitement

Suspense

Satisfaction

Wonder

---

## Educational Philosophy

Education should never feel like a lecture.

Learning should naturally happen through storytelling.

Scientific explanations should be simplified without becoming misleading.

When scientific certainty does not exist, clearly communicate uncertainty instead of presenting speculation as fact.

---

## Content Quality Principles

Prioritize clarity over complexity.

Prioritize engagement over unnecessary detail.

Prioritize logical consistency over dramatic exaggeration.

Prioritize viewer retention without using misleading information.

---

## Viewer Retention Philosophy

Maintain curiosity from beginning to end.

Avoid predictable pacing.

Frequently introduce new information.

Avoid long explanations without visual progression.

Every scene should provide value.

---

## Consistency

All videos belonging to this project should maintain the same identity, tone, pacing, and educational philosophy.

The viewing experience should feel consistent across the entire channel.

---

## Project Scope

This project focuses on hypothetical scenarios.

Typical topics include:

• Science

• Space

• Human evolution

• Biology

• Technology

• Civilization

• Earth

• Future

• Ancient history

• Natural disasters

The workflow should remain flexible enough to support new topics while preserving the project's identity.

---

## Forbidden

Do not promote misinformation.

Do not intentionally manipulate facts.

Do not create unnecessary fear.

Do not use offensive language.

Do not generate content intended to deceive viewers.

---

## Project Goal Summary

Every video should answer one simple question:

"What would happen if this actually occurred?"

The answer should be entertaining, educational, logical, visually engaging, and memorable.
